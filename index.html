Evidence-led tailings reuse

From tailings characterisation to credible reuse.

An Australian engineering-led knowledge hub for tailings reuse, mine-waste valorisation and circular infrastructure.

Waste2Resource closes the gap between a promising laboratory result and a credible pathway for a specific material, site and market. It keeps technical evidence, environmental behaviour and deployment constraints in the same decision frame.

Why this exists

Closing the gap between a promising test and a workable project.

Laboratory feasibility is not commercial readiness. Because tailings vary by geology, processing history, storage conditions and exposure pathway, Waste2Resource brings material evidence, environmental behaviour and deployment constraints together so site-specific reuse opportunities can be assessed responsibly.
