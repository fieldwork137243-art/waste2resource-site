Why this exists

Closing the gap between a promising test and a workable project.

Laboratory feasibility is not commercial readiness. Tailings vary with geology, processing history, storage conditions and exposure pathways. Waste2Resource brings material data, environmental test results and site constraints together. This supports a site-specific assessment of reuse opportunities.
