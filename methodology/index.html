Evidence methodology

Trust comes from showing how a record was made.

Waste2Resource separates source quality, evidence maturity, technical readiness and whether results apply at another site. The library is a structured evidence map. It is not a product approval list.

Transparent inclusionFive maturity levelsCorrections welcomed

Inclusion criteria

What enters the library.

A record must describe a defined mine-waste or tailings stream, a reuse, recovery or rehabilitation pathway, and a traceable public source. The library can include peer-reviewed papers, government programmes, technical conference papers and company disclosures. Each record labels its evidence basis so readers do not treat these sources as equivalent.

Every record includes a Source states note. This is a short, source-derived statement that supports the specific claim shown in the library and exposes important limitations where the source reports them. It is a faithful paraphrase unless quotation marks explicitly identify a direct quotation.

Records are excluded when the underlying project cannot be identified or the claim cannot be traced to a source. Purely promotional material is also excluded when it lacks enough technical context to assess what has been demonstrated.

Evidence maturity

Five levels describe how far the pathway has progressed.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Prospectivity, desktop assessment or proposed research; no representative performance result yet.

  2. 02

    Laboratory

    Bench-scale characterisation or product testing on a defined sample or formulation.

  3. 03

    Pilot

    Integrated process or product tested at a scale that begins to expose handling and quality-control constraints.

  4. 04

    Demonstration

    Field, site or pre-commercial deployment under realistic operating or exposure conditions.

  5. 05

    Operating

    An implemented pathway with ongoing production or use. Operating does not automatically mean independently verified or transferable.

Classification rules

Four labels answer four different questions.

01

Evidence basis

Who produced the source and what type of evidence is available: peer review, government work, conference paper or company disclosure.

02

Maturity

Where the work sits from concept to operating deployment.

03

Readiness

What the available result supports and which tests or approvals remain before use at a specific site.

04

Use elsewhere

What needs to be re-tested because residue, climate, exposure, specifications, logistics and regulation differ by site.

Record governance

Status, review and correction.

Published records have passed editorial screening and include at least one traceable source. Each record carries a last-reviewed date. A published status is not an endorsement, certification or independent technical verification.

Material corrections, stronger sources and conflicts of interest should be sent through the record’s “Suggest a correction” link or the collaboration page. Changes that alter the technical interpretation trigger a new review date.

Contributors may suggest cases, but editorial wording and inclusion decisions remain independent. Confidential or commercially sensitive information should not be submitted.
