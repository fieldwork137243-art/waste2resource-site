Technical case record · Water treatment

Tailings-derived media for nickel-mine seepage treatment

Alkali-activated material containing mine tailings and zeolite was tested in a permeable reactive barrier treating seepage water.

Northern FinlandPilotPilot evidence

What was developed or tested

A field pilot used granulated adsorbent in a 0.5 m³/day set-up, connecting material synthesis with real seepage-water performance.

What the evidence supports

A direct waste-to-treatment pathway at pilot scale. It is promising, but service life and spent-media management need longer field evidence.

Environmental and technical uncertainty

Media stability, saturation, regeneration or disposal and release of bound constituents govern lifecycle performance.

Benefit-case screening lens

Potential value: Potential value includes water recovery, a reduced discharge burden and support for compliance. Some projects may also recover metals or useful treatment products when the evidence supports that outcome.

Deployment constraints: Flow and chemistry variability, consumables, energy, plant reliability, sludge or spent-media management and long-term treatment obligations.

Question before applying elsewhere

Can treatment performance, media life, hydraulic behaviour and spent-material management be demonstrated under representative field conditions?
