Technical case record · Carbon mineralisation

SCO2UT ultramafic-tailings field research

A multi-site programme investigates accelerated carbon mineralisation and verification methods across different residues and climates.

British Columbia and Canadian mine sitesField researchConcept evidence
Source states:

The research program is testing ways to accelerate carbon mineralisation, verify storage stability and integrate field trials into mine operations.

Verify in UBC MDRU SCO2UT programme ↗

What was developed or tested

The work considers mineralogy, comminution, deposition, climate, gas concentration and operational integration rather than one universal process.

What the evidence supports

This landscape case shows why carbonation performance cannot be transferred by commodity name alone. It provides methods and field trials rather than a commercial claim.

Environmental and technical uncertainty

Potential co-benefits include dust mitigation and metal immobilisation; each site still requires water-quality and geochemical assessment.

Benefit-case screening lens

Potential value: Durable carbon removal, possible carbon value, improved residue stability and a measurable decarbonisation contribution.

Deployment constraints: Additional uptake, kinetics, measurement and verification, energy, water, land, operating integration and the permanence of storage.

Question before applying elsewhere

Can additional, durable carbon uptake be measured and verified without creating unacceptable energy, water or environmental burdens?
