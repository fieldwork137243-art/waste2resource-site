Structured evidence submission

Suggest a case for review.

Provide enough context to identify the material, pathway and evidence source. Submissions are reviewed before anything is published; submitting a record does not imply endorsement or inclusion.

  1. SubmitShare the case and a traceable source.
  2. ScreenThe record is checked for relevance and provenance.
  3. ReviewEvidence, maturity and uncertainty are summarised.
  4. PublishAccepted records receive a review date and status.

This first version opens your email app with the structured submission addressed to hello@waste2resource.com.au. No API key or personal data is stored in the website.

Editorial route

What happens next

  • Relevance and source provenance are checked
  • The evidence basis and maturity are classified
  • Uncertainty and transfer questions are retained
  • Accepted records are published as source-linked entries
