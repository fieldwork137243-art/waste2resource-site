Collaborate

Bring a case, a question or a different interpretation.

Waste2Resource is an open engineering inquiry. Technical discussion, evidence review, corrections and source-linked case suggestions are welcome.

01 / SUGGEST

Suggest evidence

Share a case study, research paper, pilot or operating example that should be considered for the library.

02 / REVIEW

Review a record

Offer a correction, stronger source or a practitioner’s interpretation of the evidence and its limitations.

03 / DISCUSS

Compare notes

Discuss a technical question on characterisation, testing, environmental behaviour or implementation.

04 / EXPLORE

Explore collaboration

Start a conversation about research, industry work, a joint technical note, panel or speaking opportunity.

Useful context

Help make the conversation specific

  • The residue or mine-waste stream
  • Location and commodity
  • The reuse or recovery pathway
  • Current evidence or project stage
  • A traceable source, where available

Evidence contributions

What makes a useful submission?

Useful contributions include enough information to check provenance and understand the engineering context. A promising claim is welcome, but publication requires a traceable source and clear distinction between research, pilot work and operating deployment.
